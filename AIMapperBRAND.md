# The AIMapper — Brand Outline

Quick-reference brand sheet for **The AIMapper** (theaimapper.com).
Values are the source of truth from the site's stylesheet (`css/style.css`).

---

## Fonts

| Use | Font | Weights | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Headings / logo** | Plus Jakarta Sans | 500, 700, 800 | Google Fonts |
| **Body / UI** | Inter | 400, 500, 600, 700 | Google Fonts |

Fallback stack: `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`

Google Fonts embed:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@500;700;800&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

## Gradients

- **Brand text gradient** (gold → indigo, ~100°): `#FACC15 → #F5B40E → #8B8DF5 → #6366F1`
  - Used on highlighted headline words.
  - CSS: `linear-gradient(100deg, #FACC15 0%, #F5B40E 25%, #8B8DF5 75%, #6366F1 100%)`
- **CTA band gradient** (purple, ~115°): `#4F46E5 → #6366F1 → #7C3AED`
  - Used on call-to-action bands.
  - CSS: `linear-gradient(115deg, #4F46E5 0%, #6366F1 55%, #7C3AED 100%)`

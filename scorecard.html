How the scorecard works

For each area of your business, answer two questions.

Time: how much of the week does it eat?

1 = a little · 2 = a fair bit · 3 = it's a drain

Repeat: how repetitive is the work?

1 = varied · 2 = somewhat · 3 = same thing daily

Your score for each area is Time × Repeat, out of 9. The highest score is your best place to start.

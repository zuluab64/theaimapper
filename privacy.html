Privacy

The AIMapper collects very little, and only what you choose to give us.

The Scorecard

You can fill in the Scorecard without giving us anything. If you enter your email and press "Send my result", we receive your email address, your scores and your answer to the autopilot question. We use them only to follow up on your result.

Booking a call

Calls are booked through Calendly, which collects the details you enter there under its own privacy policy.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

What we don't do

This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. We don't sell or share your details, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email belinda_aber@theaimapper.com.
